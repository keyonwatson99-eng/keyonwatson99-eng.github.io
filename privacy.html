Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

What Watty Bot is

Watty Bot is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, Watty Bot has read-only access to YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time and estimated revenue) for the channels the owner manages. It can't upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.

How the data is used

The data is used only to post summaries to the owner's private Discord server. It is stored only on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

Watty Bot doesn't sell, share or transfer any Google user data to anyone. It doesn't use the data for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove Watty Bot's access at any time from their Google Account under Third-party apps and services.
